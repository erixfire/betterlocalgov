# Report Illegal Dumping or Waste Management Violations in Iloilo City

Illegal dumping, burning of waste, and improper disposal are violations of the national **Ecological Solid Waste Management Act (RA 9003)** and local ordinances.

## How to report

1. Note the **exact location**, date, time, and a description of what was dumped. A photo helps.
2. Report to your **barangay hall** first.
3. If it continues, call **Iloilo City Hall** at (033) 333-1111 and ask for the General Services Office.
4. For danger to people, call the **CDRRMO** at 333 1333 / 333 2333 or the **ICPO** at 337 0400.

> The city has not published a dedicated hotline or online form for reporting dumping. The steps above use the contacts the city does publish.
