# Learn Proper Waste Segregation and Disposal Methods in Iloilo City

Under the national **Ecological Solid Waste Management Act (Republic Act 9003)**, households must segregate their waste at source. Iloilo City relies on this to cut the amount that goes to its landfill in Calajunan, Mandurriao.

## Basic segregation

| Type              | Examples                                     | What to do                                                                                                              |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Biodegradable** | Food scraps, leaves                          | Compost, or put in a separate bin for collection                                                                        |
| **Recyclable**    | Paper, plastic bottles, cans, glass          | Clean, dry and keep separate                                                                                            |
| **Residual**      | Sachets, wrappers, used tissue               | Bin for regular collection                                                                                              |
| **Special waste** | Batteries, electronics, bulbs, medical waste | Do not mix with regular garbage. See [Special Waste](/request-special-waste-collection-hazardous-materials-electronics) |

## Tips

- Keep waste dry and tied so it is not scattered.
- Put waste out only at your area's collection time (see [Collection Schedules](/check-garbage-collection-schedules-and-request-pickup)).
- Do not dump in creeks, canals or roadsides. The city has been strengthening waste management in villages along creeks.

> Specific colors for bins and local penalties are set by city and barangay ordinances. Ask your barangay hall or the General Services Office for the rules that apply in your area.
