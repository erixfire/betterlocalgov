# Request Special Waste Collection (Hazardous Materials, Electronics) in Iloilo City

Under the national **Ecological Solid Waste Management Act (RA 9003)**, electronic and electrical waste, batteries, bulbs, and similar items are classed as **special waste**. They should not be put in regular garbage.

## What to do

1. Keep special waste separate and do not burn or dump it.
2. Ask your barangay hall whether a collection or drop-off is scheduled.
3. For larger quantities, contact the **General Services Office** through City Hall at (033) 333-1111.

> The Iloilo City Government has not published a standing program or request form for special waste collection or e-waste drop-off. Contact the office above before disposing of hazardous waste.
