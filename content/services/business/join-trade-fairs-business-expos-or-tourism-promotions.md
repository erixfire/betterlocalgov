# Join Trade Fairs, Business Expos or Tourism Promotions in Iloilo City

Trade fairs, expos and investment promotions in Iloilo City are coordinated by the **Local Economic Development and Investment Promotions Office (LEDIPO)**, which is based in the Iloilo City Business Center at City Hall.

## Who to contact

| Office     | Where                                                         | Contact        |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| **LEDIPO** | Iloilo City Business Center, Iloilo City Hall, Plaza Libertad | (033) 332 1368 |

**Office hours:** Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM.

## How to take part

1. Make sure your business has a valid **Mayor's Business Permit** (see [Apply for a Business Permit](/apply-for-barangay-clearance-and-mayors-business-permits)).
2. Contact LEDIPO and ask about upcoming fairs, expos and promotion programs, and whether your business qualifies.
3. Follow the city's official announcements for calls for participants.

> The city does not publish a standing calendar or participation requirements for trade fairs on its website. Requirements, fees and schedules are set per event, so confirm them with LEDIPO.

**Sources:** [Iloilo City Government — Business Permit](https://iloilocity.gov.ph/business-permit/)
