# Renew Permits and Pay Local Business Taxes in Iloilo City

Business permit renewal in Iloilo City is done through the same online BPLS portal used for new applications.

**Online portal:** <https://bpbc.ibpls.com/cityofiloiloiloilo>

---

## How to renew

1. Log in to the portal.
2. Choose **Business Permit**, then **+ New Application**.
3. Select **Renewal** and enter your **Business Identification No. (BIN)**. The BIN is required for renewals.
4. Choose your payment mode: **Annual**, **Bi-Annual** or **Quarterly**.
5. Enter the current tax year and, if needed, tick the box to generate a **Barangay Clearance**.
6. Update the business information and enter the **previous year's gross sales or receipts** for each line of business.
7. Upload your documents and **Submit**.

### Documents to upload

- Unified Application Form (UAF)
- Declaration of Gross Receipts for the previous year
- Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC) from the Bureau of Fire Protection
- Photo of the CCTV monitor (Regulation Ordinance No. 2023-085)

---

## Paying your taxes and fees

Payments are made at the Iloilo City Business Center in City Hall, or at the city's off-site payment centers:

- 2/F Festive Walk Parade Building
- 2/F The Atrium
- 2/F Marymart Mall (food court area)
- SM City Iloilo (South Wing, lower ground floor)

**Office hours:** Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM.

> The deadline for renewal and the schedule of fees are not stated on the city's portal page. Confirm them with the Business Permit and Licensing Division before the renewal period to avoid penalties.
