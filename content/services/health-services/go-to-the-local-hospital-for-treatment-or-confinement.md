WVMC and WVSUMC are public hospitals. The others are private.

## City health services that can help with costs

- **Maayo Med** free medicines at the district health centers and the City Health Office
- **USWAG Dialysis Center** in San Isidro, Jaro and East Baluarte, Molo

## Emergencies

- **CDRRMO (ICER):** 333 1333 / 333 2333
- **Red Cross:** 337 5950
- **Police (ICPO):** 337 0400 / 166

> Admission requirements, rates and PhilHealth coverage are set by each hospital. Call the hospital first for details.
