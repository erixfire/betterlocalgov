# Learn About Supplementary Activities for Schools in Iloilo City

The city government supports learning outside the classroom through its public libraries, reading centers and school programs.

## Iloilo City Public Library branches

- Nabitasan
- Graciano Lopez Jaena
- Justice Ramon Avanceña District Library
- Quintin Salas, Jaro
- Sta. Filomina, Arevalo
- PHHC Blk 17 Reading and Study Center
- Rizal Pala-Pala II Reading and Study Center

## School support

- **Free school supplies** for public school learners from Kindergarten to Grade 12, distributed each June.
- **Uswag Scholarship** and other scholarships for college students (see [Apply for Local Scholarships](/apply-for-local-scholarships)).
- **Technical Institute of Iloilo City** with campuses in Molo and Bo. Obrero.

## Find out what is on

Schedules for reading programs, school activities and events are posted by the city government and the individual libraries. Contact the library branch near you, or the Department of Education Schools Division of Iloilo City, for current activities.
