# Apply for Local Scholarships in Iloilo City

The city's main scholarship is the **Uswag Scholarship Program**, which gives educational financial assistance and mentorship to deserving, indigent college students. It started in 2010 and has been continued under Mayor Raisa Treñas. Reports count more than 29,000 Ilonggo students helped over its first 15 years, and 3,220 cash-grant beneficiaries as of June 2026.

## Benefits

- **₱5,000 cash assistance per semester**, or **₱10,000 per year**
- Mentorship and holistic development activities

## Eligibility (as reported)

- Resident of Iloilo City; parents have lived in the city for at least two years
- Good moral character
- Family gross income not above **₱220,000 a year** for the last two years
- Minimum grade of **80%** in all subjects

## Other scholarship programs

| Program                                     | Details                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **PWD Scholarship**                         | ₱5,000 per semester for scholars with disability, handled through the city's PDAO |
| **PHINMA University of Iloilo partnership** | The city partnered with PHINMA UI for a scholarship covering 200 students         |
| **Technical Institute of Iloilo City**      | Vocational and technical courses at the Molo and Bo. Obrero campuses              |

## How to apply

1. Watch the city government's official announcements for the application period.
2. Prepare proof of residency, school records and proof of family income.
3. Submit through the office named in the announcement.

> The eligibility figures above come from news reports, and sources do not all agree. Application windows, forms and income limits can change each year. Confirm them with the City Government before you apply.
